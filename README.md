# TAIF

**Trustworthy Agentic Investment Framework**

A research project for evaluating the effectiveness of AI models in analyzing and investing in the Taiwan stock market.

---

# Research Goal

This project aims to investigate whether Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multi-Agent AI systems can improve investment analysis quality and decision-making in the Taiwan stock market.
